Synchronized motion and forces

Roll, pitch, yaw and Gx/Gy/Gz use the selected seat’s own frame. Speed and distance refer to the train centre. This display is thinned to 15 Hz; native acceptance uses 960/1920 Hz plus spatial refinement. Euler angles help inspection; physical orientation derivatives are checked separately.

Seed 42 passes the current native force, rate, energy, clearance, continuity and refinement checks, including representative front/middle/rear seats and an independent saved-file reload. These are prototype model results, not validated manufacturer hardware or a reference-intensity comparison.

Still open

  • The steeper camelback is a source study. Its whole-train loads, placement and clearance have not yet been accepted.
  • Terrain comes back next, as a shared input to planning, ground/support checks, rendering and saves. This checkpoint remains flat; it does not claim terrain-aware generation.
  • The return’s feel, signature strength, roll reversals and material variation across seeds still need review and corpus verification.
  • The segmented LSM model permits pitch changes. Actual stator/fin articulation and gap tolerances are unverified hardware assumptions.
  • No V2 packaged GPU smoke test or new Play shortcut has been completed. The original project and its Play shortcut are preserved.
