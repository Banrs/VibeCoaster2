# Support connection reference audit — 29 September 2026

The user requested external photographs/renders, specifically Falcon's Flight
and other heavy coasters. No POV footage was used for this support-head pass.

## Direct photographic evidence

- [Intamin's track-closing post, December 2024](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/intaminofficial_falconsflight-qiddiya-intamin-activity-7272989609804902400--P7p).
  The first photograph shows the cliff-side track above a large inclined support.
  A short flanged stub connects a stiffened bracket beneath the spine to the

  Local inspection copies: `out/support-study/reference/ff-closing-1.jpg` and

  Safety netting and the train obscure parts of the head; it is not sufficient
  to reconstruct the hidden bracket by itself.
- [Intamin's Hyperion photograph](https://www.intamin.com/project/hyperion/).
  The right side shows large tubular members and bolted flange joints, but the
  main track-bearing contact is partly outside the image. It supports the member

- [SFOT Source's original Tormenta ground photographs](https://www.sfotsource.com/rides/tormenta).
  The loop and Immelmann use inclined tubes and compact connections to a box
  spine. Their external support arrangement informs the inversion frames; the
  accepted round Exa spine is retained.

## Interpretation used in the model

The support retains a constant full-width column into its mating flange. Above
that flange, a compact saddle has a chamfered 60 mm seat, two 50 mm transverse
webs and two 55 mm longitudinal cheeks. The longitudinal span is 1.22 m. Those
are chosen game-art proportions, not dimensions published by Intamin. The
visible load path is spine → stiffened saddle → flanged stub → main structure.

Each weld edge samples the native curve and roll around its attachment. Local
forward and increasing native track distance are reconciled explicitly; finite
track ends shorten the welded part instead of clamping multiple vertices onto
one cross-section. Rails remain 105 mm radius and 1.40 m apart.

The images do not establish concealed welds, bearing freedom, bolt grades or
structural capacity. No claim about those details is used in the generator.
