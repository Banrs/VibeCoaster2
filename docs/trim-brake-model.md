# Track propulsion and braking

The user requested optional trims throughout the ride, realistic actuation and visible LSM/brake hardware. Automatic placement and native operating cases are verified on the reference-shaped seed-42 checkpoint. Packaged rendering remains pending.

The independent core now supports an explicit Trim operation. An upstream detector samples train speed once; the selected deployment remains latched while the train passes. A finite actuator interval precedes physical engagement. Insufficient detector lead fails with TRIM_ARMING. A spatial field taper models the entrance and exit of the brake array for each car, rather than assigning a new train speed.

The reduced characteristic is F(v) = -Fpeak * 2(v/vpeak)/(1+(v/vpeak)^2). It opposes motion, is linear near rest, reaches the rated peak at vpeak and falls at high speed. Its power asymptote is 2 Fpeak vpeak, which the configured rating must cover. Eddy braking cannot hold a stationary vehicle. This characteristic follows the qualitative force-speed model discussed in [Thompson's permanent-magnet brake paper](https://thompsonrd.com/OSEE-brakes.pdf); the prototype coefficients are not calibrated Intamin hardware data.

Upstream sensing and an actuated magnetic configuration are documented in [EP3793074B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3793074B1/en). [Intamin's account](https://www.intamin.com/company/about-us/) also describes conductive blades passing between permanent magnets. These references support the mechanism, not a claim that this implementation reproduces a proprietary brake assembly.

Force and dissipated work act on the finite train in the normal RK4 replay. Focused tests cover a separate analytic speed-distance solution, zero-speed and reversed-motion behaviour, inactive/deployed brakes, short sensor lead rejection, finite-train occupancy, energy conservation and 960/1920 Hz agreement. TRIM_BRAKES persists characteristic speed and sensor lead in the COASTER6 extension directory. The reference-shaped seed-42 save independently reloads with generated trims. Disabled, all-deployed, and 20%-lower-drag cases pass native force/performance/refinement checks; per-zone work and peak power are retained in camelback-traced-trim-cases.log. These cases are not a complete operating envelope or thermal model.

The renderer now receives segmented stators, conductive fins/actuator housings and friction-brake assemblies from the same canonical operations. Native tests check their intervals, track frames and containment within the reserved nonlocal-clearance envelope. Unreal compilation/GPU appearance, own-car mechanical interfaces, detailed gap/thermal calibration are still pending. Automatic placement selects clear unpowered climbing corridors, and nominal-speed trains leave the fins retracted. The Unreal source animates fin deployment from the native controller observations; distinct motor and brake materials are prepared but not GPU-verified. No F2291 compliance claim follows from these tests.
