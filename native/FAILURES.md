# Rejected 0.8.1–0.8.3 work

Reported failures: geometrically inaccurate and partial elements, abrupt joins, jerky force authoring, too many weak LSMs, and excessive terrain clearance (reported average over 200 m).

Numerical acceptance and repeatability were treated as evidence of good geometry and ride feel. Oversized verification loops delayed visual/user review and accumulated unnecessary code, tooling and notes.

The improved Immelmann was retained; the rest was rolled back to 0.8.0.
